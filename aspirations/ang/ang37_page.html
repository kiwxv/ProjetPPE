Company Car Leasing & Salary Sacrifice Experts

From Car leasing to Salary Sacrifice, we provide solutions to all business and personal customers. FleetSauce was founded in order to provide the highest level of customer service, we arm our customers with the information they need so they are able to make informed decisions about all aspects of car & van leasing.

We pride ourselves on being open and honest in all areas of business, if you need advice we will do our utmost to ensure you receive the best and most relevant information. Our dedicated Account Manager team are on hand to answer any questions from choosing a vehicle all the way through to the end of its contract and beyond.

As well as offering highly competitive Car & Van lease pricing we also provide a wide range of other services including Fleet Solutions, Salary Sacrifice, Accident Management and advice on legal compliancy. All Fleetsauce customers have access to our online Fleet Management system which is capable of storing all of your fleets’ details, including those sourced elsewhere.

We would be happy to discuss your requirements with you or take any challenge to beat an existing quote you have so please feel free to contact us.
