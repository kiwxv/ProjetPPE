Fleet Management Services

Comprehensive services address all your fleet needs: maintenance, accident management, driver safety, fuel and EV charging, tolling, vehicle telematics, driver tax compliance and more.

Sustainable Fleet Solutions for a Greener Future

As businesses increasingly prioritize sustainability, Wheels is committed to helping you transition to an environmentally friendly fleet.  We offer comprehensive fleet management solutions for electric vehicles (EVs), including:

  • Vehicle Selection and Acquisition: Our experts can guide you through the process of selecting and acquiring the right EVs for your needs.
  • Charging Infrastructure: We can help you plan and implement the necessary charging infrastructure to support your EV fleet.
  • Driver Training: We provide comprehensive driver training programs to ensure your team is prepared to operate and maintain EVs.
  • Software Integration: Our fleet management software seamlessly integrates with EVs, providing real-time monitoring, charging status updates, and performance data.
