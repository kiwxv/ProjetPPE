FAQs

What is a Fleet Management Solution?

A fleet management solution brings together telematics and fleet management software so that organisations can better oversee their vehicles and increase efficiency, cost control, and safety through vehicle tracking, driver behaviour monitoring, maintenance scheduling, compliance support, reporting, and data analysis.

What is Fleet Telematics?

Fleet telematics is a technology used to manage vehicle fleets through real-time data collection by combining GPS with onboard diagnostics, continuously monitoring location, speed, fuel consumption, driving habits, and various other operational details to help fleet managers make informed decisions.

What are the Benefits of a Fleet Telematics System?

A fleet telematics system offers a wide range of advantages by delivering detailed performance metrics. Specifically, managers can streamline routes, cut down on fuel costs, and stay on top of maintenance needs before issues arise. Driver safety is likewise improved through monitoring and feedback, and accurate tracking enhances customer service with precise ETAs.

Is Fleet Telematics the Same as Fleet Tracking?

No, fleet tracking primarily focuses on the location of vehicles. Telematics, on the other hand, offers a comprehensive view that includes vehicle performance, driver behaviour, fuel usage, and maintenance needs. Therefore, tracking tells you where your vehicles are, and telematics explains how they’re being driven in order to maintain a safer, more compliant, and cost-effective fleet.

Which Industries use Fleet Management Solutions?

Regardless of a business’s or company’s size, fleet management solutions are in use within a wide range of sectors, including transport and logistics, courier and delivery, public transportation, cold chain, construction, as well as waste and recycling.
