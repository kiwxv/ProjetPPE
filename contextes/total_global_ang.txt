Qu'est-ce que le fleet management ?

   Fleet management : tout savoir Fleet management : tout savoir Fleet
   management : tout savoir Fleet management : tout savoir Fleet
   management : tout savoir Fleet management : tout savoir

   bridgestone fleetcare bridgestone fleetcare white

   Le fleet management (ou gestion de flotte) regroupe les processus
   administratifs d'acquisition, d'entretien, d'organisation et de revente
   des véhicules commerciaux. Elle inclut également la surveillance en
   temps réel de l'état des véhicules, de leur position géographique et
   des comportements de conduite des conducteurs. Le fleet management peut
   également faire référence aux composants matériels et logiciels
   nécessaires à la configuration des fonctionnalités de surveillance et
   de géolocalisation indispensables à une gestion adéquate de votre parc.

Quels sont les avantages du fleet management ?

   Quels sont les avantages du fleet management ?

   Le fleet management est devenu un élément clé pour les entreprises qui
   disposent de véhicules commerciaux. Le principal avantage du fleet
   management est la possibilité de surveiller et d'analyser leur flotte
   dans le détail.
--
   Cela permet de nombreuses optimisations très précises, qui s'ajoutent
   les unes aux autres et améliorent significativement l'efficacité. Outre
   les avantages économiques évidents pour le fleet manager, il permet de
   mieux comprendre le fonctionnement de la flotte dans son ensemble. Il
   vous permettra notamment d’optimiser vos coûts de carburant ou le temps
--
   de meilleures décisions, avec davantage de transparence.

Technologie de fleet management

   Technologie de fleet management

   Le fleet management inclut une large gamme de composants matériels et
   logiciels, qui permettent de mesurer, d'enregistrer, de traiter et de
   transmettre à un serveur d'importants volumes de données pertinentes en
   temps réel et d’en faciliter la gestion pour le fleet manager. Ces
   composants comprennent notamment :
     * Un système de géolocalisation par GPS dans chaque véhicule de votre
--
       cellulaire ou satellite ;
     * Un serveur, qui traite toutes les données et les renvoie aux
       appareils et applications de fleet management connectés ;
     * Un logiciel de fleet management pour l'utilisateur final (fleet
       manager ou gestionnaire de flotte), qui affiche toutes ces
       informations de manière claire.

   En s'appuyant sur cette association étroite de différentes
   technologies, le logiciel de fleet management est capable d'afficher
   des données en temps réel sur l'ensemble de la flotte.

Le fleet management, une solution qui s'adapte à toutes les entreprises

   Le fleet management, une solution qui s'adapte à toutes les entreprises

   De plus en plus d'entreprises de transport, notamment les entreprises
   des secteurs du transport par poids lourd, avion ou bateau et de la
   livraison, adoptent le fleet management pour gérer leur flotte et
   dématérialiser encore davantage leur approche des opérations à grande
   échelle. L'un des principaux aspects du fleet management est sa
   capacité à s'adapter à toutes les entreprises, quelle que soit leur
   taille. En outre, la rentabilité de ce système augmente avec l'échelle
   de mise en œuvre.

   Les logiciels de fleet management vous permettent de visualiser la
   position physique et l'état de tous les véhicules d'une flotte ainsi
   que le comportement des conducteurs d'un simple coup d'œil. Vous pouvez
   facilement et rapidement obtenir des informations sur un véhicule
   donné, et ce à tout moment. Les opérations à grande échelle tireront le
   meilleur parti d'une solution de fleet management. Toutefois, cette
   dernière peut également améliorer considérablement l'efficacité
   d'entreprises plus petites.

FAQs

   What is a Fleet Management Solution?

   A fleet management solution brings together telematics and fleet
   management software so that organisations can better oversee their
   vehicles and increase efficiency, cost control, and safety through
--
   compliance support, reporting, and data analysis.

   What is Fleet Telematics?

   Fleet telematics is a technology used to manage vehicle fleets through
   real-time data collection by combining GPS with onboard diagnostics,
   continuously monitoring location, speed, fuel consumption, driving
   habits, and various other operational details to help fleet managers
   make informed decisions.

   What are the Benefits of a Fleet Telematics System?

   A fleet telematics system offers a wide range of advantages by
   delivering detailed performance metrics. Specifically, managers can
   streamline routes, cut down on fuel costs, and stay on top of
--
   enhances customer service with precise ETAs.

   Is Fleet Telematics the Same as Fleet Tracking?

   No, fleet tracking primarily focuses on the location of vehicles.
   Telematics, on the other hand, offers a comprehensive view that
   includes vehicle performance, driver behaviour, fuel usage, and
   maintenance needs. Therefore, tracking tells you where your vehicles
   are, and telematics explains how they’re being driven in order to
   maintain a safer, more compliant, and cost-effective fleet.

Fleet Register

   The EU Fleet Register is a database where all the fishing vessels
   flying the flag of an EU country have to be be registered. Any changes
   in the status of a fishing vessel, for example if it has been scrapped,
   need to be registered by the member country in the Fleet Register.

   The main objective of the Fleet Register is to enable any EU fishing
   vessel with key characteristics to be identified, with the aim of:
     * Monitoring the implementation of capacity management countries
--
       Commission and EU countries in charge of control and inspection
     * Serving as an accurate source of statistical data on the European
       fishing fleet
     * Serving as a reference database for vessels characteristics for
       other applications that manage information on fishing vessels

   The data contained in the Fleet Register can be classified in three
   categories:
     * Administrative identifications Vessel name, registration port,
--
     * Technical characteristics Length, tonnage, power, fishing gear,
       etc.
     * Historical events Entry into and exit from the fleet, modifications
       of characteristics

What is fleet management?

   Fleet management encompasses the systems, processes and tools needed to
   ensure an organization’s fleet of business vehicles are maintained
   throughout their entire lifecycle.

   It also involves the management of fleet drivers, optimization of fleet
   operations, and improvement of asset utilization—all of which fall
   under the larger umbrella of enterprise asset management.

   A fleet can range from commercial vehicles to freight trucks to
   airplanes, depending on the organization. Some organizations may build
   their own fleet while others may hire fleet management companies. With
   proper fleet management, organizations can improve efficiency, reduce
   costs and ensure that their business is complying with the latest
   regulations.

What are the benefits of fleet management?

   A smart fleet management system can help organizations achieve their
   environmental, social and governance (ESG) goals. For instance, fleet
   providers can play a crucial role in minimizing their organization’s
   economic footprint by tracking fuel consumption and other diagnostic
   data. In doing so, they can also improve fleet performance by
   preventing vehicles from breaking down and avoiding costly vehicle
   acquisitions.

   Fleet management can also enable more positive vehicle inspection
   outcomes. A poor vehicle inspection can ground vehicles, even when they
   are fully operational. Fleet management can provide either the
   predictive or preventative maintenance that keeps vehicles in
   circulation.

   Finally, by implementing various fleet management solutions, companies
   can improve upkeep, avoid last-minute cancellations and increase the
   number of customers they serve. This can result in more satisfied

What does a fleet manager do?

   Fleet managers oversee fleet operations while maintaining fleet
   efficiency. At a glance, that may seem like routine asset performance
   management or fleet maintenance. However, a fleet manager’s
   responsibilities include everything from:

Acquiring vehicles

   Fleet managers determine when a new vehicle is needed, what
   requirements the vehicle must meet, and whether an older vehicle needs
   to be replaced.

Maintaining the fleet

   Fleet managers are responsible for preventative vehicle maintenance to
   avoid breakdowns, balancing maintenance costs with maintenance
   schedules and handling repairs so the fleet remains operational.

Managing drivers

   Fleet managers recruit drivers, manage their workflows and work to
   retain talent. They also ensure driver safety by implementing proper
   training, monitoring driver behavior and optimizing safety procedures.
--
Implementing fuel programs

   Fleet managers are responsible for fuel management, which includes
   mapping out preferred fueling locations, creating processes to track
   fuel usage and lowering fueling costs for the entire vehicle fleet.

Complying with regulations

   Fleet managers oversee hours of service (HOS), electronic logging
   devices (ELDs) and other regulatory requirements to ensure that all
   drivers are operating in full compliance.
--
Reducing operational costs

   Fleet managers compare fleet costs, aggregate data in a central
   location and perform route optimization to improve cost savings and
   reduce downtime. Many use fleet management solutions or fleet
   management software to automate and optimize these tasks.

What is fleet management software?

   Fleet management software provides fleet managers with a real-time
   logistics platform that can orchestrate everything from fleet tracking
   and driver management to weather and traffic forecasting. Fleet
   management solutions can also improve fuel usage, provide key vehicle
   metrics and fleet data, ensure compliance with regulatory requirements
   and ultimately, optimize fleet operations.

   While similar to a transportation management system that allows a
   company to manage its inbound and outbound shipping, fleet management
   software differs in that it’s more robust and focuses solely on the
   commercial fleet.

   Some common features of fleet management software include:
     * Vehicle tracking: With GPS and OEM telematics, fleet managers are
       gaining real-time visibility into their fleet while it’s on the
       road. They can monitor vehicle locations, analyze driver
       performance and mitigate potential maintenance issues through
--
       score or rating. For example, frequent braking or idling can impact
       scores negatively. Some organizations may also use dash cams in the
       cabin of their vehicles to keep an eye on their commercial fleet.

     * Fuel management: Fuel efficiency can lead to significant cost
       reductions. Fleet management software monitors fuel costs and track
       vehicles’ fuel consumption. It may also automate the refueling
       process to avoid unnecessary refueling or worse, running out of
       fuel.

     * Route planning: Fleet managers will often plan routes in advance
       for optimal efficiency. Through a combination of GPS tracking and
       telematics, fleet managers can precisely map routes, reducing fuel
       costs while improving sustainability efforts.

     * Software updates: The rise of electric vehicles means that fleet
       operators may be expected to have a comprehensive plan to manage
       updates to vehicle operating systems, including when to update
       software and how to roll out the updates.

Where to start with fleet management

   Organizations looking to stand up a strong fleet management program
   should consider these factors:
   Pricing

   A forward-facing fleet management strategy requires significant
   investment out of the gate. Some initial costs include adding
   telematics to vehicles, purchasing or licensing software and reskilling
   the workforce.
   Fleet complexity

   Fleet sizes vary and a fleet of vehicles can range from e-bikes to
   airplanes. Large fleets with a wide variety of vehicles will require
   different approaches to each type of vehicle, especially when it comes
   to preventative maintenance.

Features and benefits

   Fleets provide the following features and benefits, enabling you to
   maximize cost savings and optimize availability and performance when
   running applications on multiple EC2 instances.

   Multiple instance types
          A fleet can launch multiple instance types, ensuring it isn't
          dependent on the availability of any single instance type. This
          increases the overall availability of instances in your fleet.

   Distributing instances across Availability Zones
          A fleet can launch into multiple Availability Zones, enabling
          you to reduce costs and improve availability. If your fleet
          includes Spot Instances, the fleet automatically selects
          Availability Zones based on your preferences regarding price and
          interruptions.

   Multiple purchasing options
          A fleet can launch multiple purchase options (Spot and On-Demand
          Instances), allowing you to optimize costs through Spot Instance
          usage. You can also take advantage of Reserved Instance and
          Savings Plans discounts by using them in conjunction with
          On-Demand Instances in the fleet.

   Automated replacement of Spot Instances
          If your fleet includes Spot Instances, it can automatically
          request replacement Spot capacity if your Spot Instances are
          interrupted. Through Capacity Rebalancing, a fleet can also
          monitor and proactively replace your Spot Instances that are at
          an elevated risk of interruption.

   Reserve On-Demand capacity
          A fleet can use an On-Demand Capacity Reservation to reserve
          On-Demand capacity. A fleet can also include Capacity Blocks for
          ML, allowing you to reserve GPU instances on a future date to
          support short duration machine learning (ML) workloads.

Learn more about fleet telematics

   What is fleet telematics?

   Fleet telematics technology is a fleet management system designed to be
   the single command center for physical operations, enabling
   organizations to streamline every aspect of their fleet, field, and
   back-office operations.

   With fleet telematics, you can:
     * Unleash employee productivity with intuitive and personalized
       workflows for your drivers, admins, and technicians.
     * Minimize risk by delivering rich safety, compliance, maintenance,
       and security insights you can act on immediately to protect your
       fleet.
     * Maximize energy efficiency with our advanced fuel, energy, and
       emissions reporting and alerting.

   How does a fleet telematics solution help improve fleet management?

   With a fleet telematics solution like Samsara help improve the safety,
   compliance, and efficiency of your fleet. With GPS tracking for
   vehicles and assets, ELD compliance, and more, Samsara is a
   comprehensive telematics system that helps streamline fleet management.

   What are the benefits of a fleet telematics system?

   There are many benefits to a fleet telematics system, including:
     * Minimize fleet risk: Protect your people, assets, and your bottom
       line with actionable safety, compliance, maintenance, and security
       insights.
--
       advanced fuel analytics, energy management, and emissions planning.

   Is fleet telematics the same as GPS tracking?

   Fleet telematics software enables organizations to track the location
   of their fleet using real-time data, including to-the-second GPS data.
   With real-time GPS location tracking, fleets gain best-in-class
   visibility to improve route performance, fleet efficiency, and customer
   service.

   How does fleet telematics help ensure ELD compliance?

   Amidst evolving regulations, fleet telematics systems are critical to
   helping fleet maintain compliance. Using fleet telematics, you gain
   real-time visibility into driver status, making it easy for drivers to
   log HOS and preventing ELD mandate violations. In addition, automated
   ELD workflows for drivers and robust reporting for admins across HOS,
   DVIR, and IFTA enable fleets to sail through audits with ease.

   A naval fleet is the largest operational formation of warships in a
   navy, typically under a single command and organized for strategic
   missions. While modern fleets are permanent, multi-role forces (e.g.,
   carrier strike groups), historical fleets were often ad hoc assemblies
   for specific campaigns.^[1] The term "fleet" can also synonymously
   refer to a nation’s entire navy, particularly in smaller maritime
   forces.^[2]

   Fleets have shaped geopolitics since antiquity—from the trireme fleets
   of Athens to the nuclear-powered carrier groups of today—enabling power
   projection, trade protection, and deterrence.^[3] Multinational fleets,
   such as NATO’s Standing Maritime Groups, demonstrate their continued
   diplomatic-military role.^[4]

Ancient and medieval fleets

   [edit]

   The earliest organized naval fleets emerged in the Eastern
   Mediterranean and East Asia, where maritime trade routes and coastal
   warfare necessitated centralized naval power.

       fleets, using biremes to dominate Levantine trade routes.^[5] Their
       shipbuilding techniques were later adopted by Greek city-states.

     * Rome: The Classis Britannica patrolled Britain’s coasts,^[7] while

       ad hoc fleets to permanent provincial squadrons.^[8]

East Asia

   [edit]
     * China: The Han dynasty (206 BCE–220 CE) deployed riverine fleets to

Common questions and answers about fleet management


   What is fleet management?

   Fleet management is the process of managing an organisation’s
   commercial vehicles, ensuring the optimal performance of those
   vehicles, and ensuring the fleet operation is efficient and
   cost-effective. This can include but is not limited to leasing and
   funding of vehicles, driver management, fuel management, accident
   management, and fleet risk assessments.

   What does a fleet management company do?

   A fleet management company helps companies streamline the running of
   their vehicles through the provision of consultancy and technology
   which can provide real-time fleet intelligence, improve fleet
   efficiency, and ultimately, reduce fleet costs. Fleet Alliance
   guarantees the cheapest lease rentals available on the market for every
   single vehicle we provide through our unique competitive tendering
   model.

   Why is fleet management important?

   Fleet management is typically one of the largest company costs.
   However, when managed effectively fleet management can significantly
   reduce the financial burden of running a fleet of vehicles, whilst
   ensuring maximum efficiency and a reduction in administration. In
   essence, fleet management provides companies with greater control of
   their fleet for less effort, whilst reducing overall costs. That’s why
   it’s a win-win for our clients.

   Who uses fleet management?

   Fleet management services are used by companies of all sizes across all
   sectors. This includes construction, oil and gas, engineering,
   security, energy, pharmaceutical, and even non-profit industries. You
   can view a sample of company testimonials here.

   How do fleet management companies work?

   Fleet management companies essentially remove the cost and hassle of
   managing your fleet leaving you and your team free to look after your
   customers and grow your business. All fleet-related suppliers are
   handled by the fleet management company directly, saving you time and
   eliminating time-consuming administration.  A fleet management company
   will also work with you to understand your objectives and demonstrate
   how different funding methods and solutions could reduce costs whilst
   supporting your company’s business ambitions.
