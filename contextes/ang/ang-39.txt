Fleet DNA: Commercial Fleet Vehicle Operating Data

   Fleet DNA logo

   The Fleet DNA database of commercial fleet vehicle operating data helps
   vehicle manufacturers and developers optimize vehicle designs and helps
   fleet managers choose advanced technologies.

   A subset of data shown in this online tool provides data summaries and
   visualizations similar to real-world "genetics" for medium- and
   heavy-duty fleet vehicles, which helps users understand the broad
   operational range of commercial vehicles across vocations and weight
   classes.

   FleetDNA is part of the FleetREDI platform.

How To Cite the Data

   Publications that use data from Fleet DNA should include a citation
   consistent with the following format:

     "Fleet DNA Project Data." ([YEAR]). NLR. Accessed [DATE]:
     www.nrel.gov/fleetdna
