Our finance options

Funding options for every business

Whatever your business goals are, Alphabet provides financial solutions that can be tailored to your operational needs. 

Finance Lease

A Finance Lease lets you keep vehicles on your balance sheet while enjoying fixed, flexible payments, with the option at the end to sell to a third party or continue using them under a peppercorn agreement (a percentage of the original invoice price).

Salary Sacrifice

Offer employees a cost-efficient car benefit that’s easy to implement, with full support from our expert team and access to a wide range of low and zero-emission vehicles.

Sale and Leaseback

This can give your business the immediate financial advantage of releasing the value tied up in your owned vehicles.

Enterprise fleet management is a balancing act covering cost control, driver satisfaction, emissions targets and operational reliability all while keeping your business mobile. 

A corporate fleet is a collection of vehicles such as cars, vans, or specialised equipment, that a business or organisation owns or leases to support its operations and transport goods, services, or people. 

The size of an enterprise fleet can vary greatly depending on the type of business, but generally they can consist of anything from 1 to 10,000 cars. 

Enterprise fleet management software is a suite of digital tools designed to assist in the management of the vehicles. They provide a range of functions to improve decision making and operations including tracking, maintenance, driver monitoring, fuel management and reporting.
