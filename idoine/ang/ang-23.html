About Fleet

Regularly voted as one of the most desirable places to live in the UK, Fleet is a warm and welcoming town which truly has a community spirit at its heart. Fleet is the perfect destination for an evening out – with a choice of independent restaurants and entertainment in the form of our very own venue, The Harlington. Alternatively, take a picturesque stroll around Fleet Pond, along Basingstoke Canal or one of our award-winning Parks.
