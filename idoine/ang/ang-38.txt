        <h2 id="ec2-fleet-features-and-benefits">Features and benefits</h2>
        <p>Fleets provide the following features and benefits, enabling you to maximize cost
            savings and optimize availability and performance when running applications on multiple
            EC2 instances.</p>
        <div class="variablelist">
             
             
             
             
             
        <dl>
                <dt><b><span class="term">Multiple instance types</span></b></dt>
                <dd>
                    <p>A fleet can launch multiple instance types, ensuring it isn't dependent on
                        the availability of any single instance type. This increases the overall
                        availability of instances in your fleet.</p>
                </dd>
            
                <dt><b><span class="term">Distributing instances across Availability Zones</span></b></dt>
                <dd>
                    <p>A fleet can launch into multiple Availability Zones, enabling you to
                        reduce costs and improve availability. If your fleet includes Spot Instances, the
                        fleet automatically selects Availability Zones based on your preferences
                        regarding price and interruptions.</p>
                </dd>
            
                <dt><b><span class="term">Multiple purchasing options</span></b></dt>
                <dd>
                    <p>A fleet can launch multiple purchase options (Spot and On-Demand Instances), allowing
                        you to optimize costs through Spot Instance usage. You can also take advantage of
                        Reserved Instance and Savings Plans discounts by using them in conjunction with On-Demand Instances in
                        the fleet. </p>
                </dd>
            
                <dt><b><span class="term">Automated replacement of Spot Instances</span></b></dt>
                <dd>
                    <p>If your fleet includes Spot Instances, it can automatically request replacement
                        Spot capacity if your Spot Instances are interrupted. Through <a href="./ec2-fleet-capacity-rebalance.html">Capacity Rebalancing</a>, a
                        fleet can also monitor and proactively replace your Spot Instances that are at an
                        elevated risk of interruption.</p>
                </dd>
            
                <dt><b><span class="term">Reserve On-Demand capacity</span></b></dt>
                <dd>
                    <p>A fleet can use an <a href="./ec2-fleet-on-demand-capacity-reservations.html">On-Demand Capacity Reservation</a> to
                        reserve On-Demand capacity. A fleet can also include <a href="./ec2-capacity-blocks.html">Capacity Blocks for ML</a>, allowing you to reserve
                        GPU instances on a future date to support short duration machine
                        learning (ML) workloads.</p>
                </dd>
            </dl></div>
