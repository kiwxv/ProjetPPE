   FAQs

Learn more about fleet telematics

   What is fleet telematics?

   Fleet telematics technology is a fleet management system designed to be
   the single command center for physical operations, enabling
   organizations to streamline every aspect of their fleet, field, and
   back-office operations.

   With fleet telematics, you can:
     * Unleash employee productivity with intuitive and personalized
       workflows for your drivers, admins, and technicians.
     * Minimize risk by delivering rich safety, compliance, maintenance,
       and security insights you can act on immediately to protect your
       fleet.
     * Maximize energy efficiency with our advanced fuel, energy, and
       emissions reporting and alerting.

   How does a fleet telematics solution help improve fleet management?

   With a fleet telematics solution like Samsara help improve the safety,
   compliance, and efficiency of your fleet. With GPS tracking for
   vehicles and assets, ELD compliance, and more, Samsara is a
   comprehensive telematics system that helps streamline fleet management.

   What are the benefits of a fleet telematics system?

   There are many benefits to a fleet telematics system, including:
     * Minimize fleet risk: Protect your people, assets, and your bottom
       line with actionable safety, compliance, maintenance, and security
       insights.
     * Increase worker productivity: Empower your teams with intuitive,
       personalized mobile app workflows for drivers, admins, and
       technicians.
     * Maximize energy efficiency: Future-proof your operations with
       advanced fuel analytics, energy management, and emissions planning.

   Is fleet telematics the same as GPS tracking?

   Fleet telematics software enables organizations to track the location
   of their fleet using real-time data, including to-the-second GPS data.
   With real-time GPS location tracking, fleets gain best-in-class
   visibility to improve route performance, fleet efficiency, and customer
   service.

   Some key features of real-time GPS tracking include:
     * Geofencing: Automatically get notified, via text or email when your
       assets arrive and leave specific locations, or when abnormal
       activity occurs.
     * Helicopter view: Eliminate guesswork with an instant aerial view of
       your assets. Easily aid route navigation and help authorities track
       stolen property.
     * Smart map overlays: Monitor weather and traffic or locate the
       nearest charging station. Quickly adapt to changing conditions so
       your drivers can stay on schedule.
     * Live location sharing: Deliver best-in-class customer service with
       live location links to equip your customers with accurate ETAs.

   How does fleet telematics help ensure ELD compliance?

   Amidst evolving regulations, fleet telematics systems are critical to
   helping fleet maintain compliance. Using fleet telematics, you gain
   real-time visibility into driver status, making it easy for drivers to
   log HOS and preventing ELD mandate violations. In addition, automated
   ELD workflows for drivers and robust reporting for admins across HOS,
   DVIR, and IFTA enable fleets to sail through audits with ease.

   How does fleet telematics software streamline preventative maintenance?

   By leveraging real-time vehicle diagnostics, fleets are able to improve
   preventative maintenance and reduce downtime. Proactively spot issues
   such as failing batteries and engine fault codes through over-the-air
   vehicle diagnostics. Avoid road breakdowns and quickly identify
   vehicles in need of service to protect vehicle health and extend
   vehicle lifetime.

   Fleet telematics systems like Samsara offer intuitive mobile workflows
   and to help improve vehicle maintenance. Samsara Vehicle Telematics
   help you prevent incidents before they happen with visibility into key
   driver and vehicle safety indicators like vehicle location, speed,
   harsh braking, and fault codes, while our Driver App helps you stay on
   top of potential defects with DVIRs.

   In addition, Samsara’s offers a robust ecosystem of integrations,
   making it easy to connect your data to existing systems to ensure
   reporting accuracy, maximize administrative efficiency, and make
   smarter, informed decisions.

   How does fleet telematics technology improve fuel management?

   With fleet telematics, you gain real-time insights that enable you to
   reduce fuel consumption and improve energy efficiency. Samsara Vehicle
   Telematics helps you:
     * Motivate drivers to idle less and save more with personalized
       scoring and performance insights.
     * Cut down on fuel waste, energy use, and emissions with actionable
       alerts and advanced reporting.
     * As EV and emissions mandates become more prevalent, Samsara’s
       automated EV assessments and customizable suitability criteria help
       you identify a path forward to vehicle electrification.
