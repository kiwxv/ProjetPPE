   Welcome

World-Class Fleet Management Services

   With the support of our teams of global professionals, we are shaping
   the future of ship management, ensuring that the goods and materials
   powering global economies are delivered swiftly and securely to their
   destinations.

   We are custodians of our clients' vessels and cargo, looking after them
   as if they were our own. A combination of experience, reliability,
   innovation and sheer passion have enabled us to become one of the
   world's leading providers of ship management services.

   Three main attributes form the core of our business: an unparalleled
   commitment to our people both onshore and at sea, client partnerships
   led by integrity and transparency, and a commitment to building the
   most innovative ship management company in the world.

People: Empowering Our Global Workforce

   Seafaring is in our DNA. From our crews to our Chairman, maritime trade
   is what drives us. It is a passion shared by our commitment to the
   27,000 seafarers and 1,200 shore-based colleagues we have working
   across 24 offices in 10 countries. Their commitment to excellence and
   dedication to their roles are the foundation of the work we do for our
   clients.
   Our commitment to transparent partnerships, alongside operational
   insights and a deep passion for protecting their assets which allows us
   to foster enduring relationships that thrive on shared goals and help
   shape the future of global maritime operations.
   We are investing heavily to equip the next-generation of seafarers with
   the skills and knowledge to excel in tomorrow's maritime industry. From
   sea to shore, we integrate the wellbeing and professional development
   of our teams into every aspect of our operations, ensuring that vessels
   operate seamlessly and meet our clients' evolving needs.
   Our talent development programmes provide comprehensive training,
   education, and mentoring opportunities, to equip our seafarers and
   employees with the skills needed to navigate an industry marked by
   digitalisation, automation, and regulatory requirements.
   Fleet Care also offers best-in-class seafarer wellbeing programmes,
   including family-centred benefits, mental health support from on-staff
   psychologists, and other resources that ensure our teams are fully
   supported.

Partnerships: Tailored Solutions for Long-Term Success

   We recognise that our role now extends beyond traditional ship
   management; our clients need partners and innovators as we navigate
   increasingly complex industry challenges together.  We are partners in
   your growth and success. Our commitment to clients is anchored in
   integrity, with a long-term view of relationships and investments. This
   focus ensures optimal fleet performance and the flexibility to adapt to
   industry shifts. Many of our client partnerships span decades, built on
   mutual trust, transparent communication, and the ability to navigate
   global challenges together.
   The size of our operations - with more than 650+ ships under Technical
   Management and New Building Supervision services - provides us with the
   insight to identify trends early, enabling us to offer our clients
   fresh perspectives and solutions that anticipate emerging needs. Many
   of our client partnerships span decades, built on mutual trust,
   transparent communication, and the ability to navigate global
   challenges together.
   As the maritime industry evolves, we remain committed to helping our
   clients transition toward a zero-carbon future. By working hand-in-hand
   with partners, we integrate sustainable practices and future-focused
   strategies, ensuring that together we can navigate towards a more
   sustainable shipping industry.

Performance: Driving Global Economic Growth with Operational Excellence

   With a 240+ strong technical and operations team, we maintain vessels
   to the highest standards, ensuring compliance and operational
   excellence. From routine maintenance to managing complex technical
   challenges, we handle every aspect of vessel operations to ensure ships
   remain compliant with international regulations. Assisting vessels
   around the world, our 80+ QHSE (Quality, Health, Safety, and
   Environment) professionals help our crews to uphold the highest safety
   standards.
   Our focus on proactive maintenance helps reduce downtime while
   increasing efficiency and reliability, and delivering the performance
   our clients expect. With strategically positioned technical and crewing
   hubs, we deliver round-the-clock support. This combination of global
   scale and local expertise allows us to respond quickly and effectively
   to client needs, ensuring that vessels operate smoothly, no matter
   where they are in the world.
   Every vessel we manage plays a crucial role in transporting essential
   goods and raw materials that sustain industries worldwide. Whether it's
   energy, agriculture, or consumer goods, we play our part in ensuring
   supply chains remain uninterrupted, helping businesses and communities
   thrive in today's interconnected global economy.
