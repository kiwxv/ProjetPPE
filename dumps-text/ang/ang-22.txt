Fleet

Distinctive literary publishing


   Fleet was launched in 2016 as a home for great literary fiction and
   non-fiction, publishing eight to ten titles a year. Dynamic,
   forward-thinking and focused on bringing the very best books to
   readers, in the short time that Fleet has been running our books have
   made a huge impact: fiction from literary powerhouses such as Clare
   Messud, Virginia Baily, Paula McLean and Colson Whitehead as well as
   affecting and important non-fiction from authors including Hope Jahren,
   Michelle Dean and Ariel Levy. Now a well-established list, we at Fleet
   are also keen to search out the new voices of the future, including
   Yara Rodrigues Fowler, Adelle Stripe, Séamas O'Reilly and Ronan Farrow.
