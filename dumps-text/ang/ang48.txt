Wherever You Need To Go, Our Fleet Services Move You Forward

   We work to optimize every part of your mobility program. From fleet
   leasing, maintenance and fuel to telematics, pool management and
   mileage reimbursement, Wheels’ fleet services support all your business
   needs, helping you improve efficiency across your fleet operations.

Vehicle Acquisitions & Upfitting

   Strategic guidance helps you procure and configure the right vehicles
   for your business.

Remarketing

   Our proven, multi-channel selling strategy delivers optimal resale
   results.

Leasing

   Cost-effective leasing solutions free up your company’s capital and
   deliver tax benefits.

Reimbursement

   Customizable payments, centralized reporting, and safety programs give
   you control and peace of mind with your mileage reimbursement drivers.

Shared Mobility

   Flexible solutions support your mobile workforce while maintaining
   oversight and cost controls.

Fleet Management Services

   Comprehensive services address all your fleet needs: maintenance,
   accident management, driver safety, fuel and EV charging, tolling,
   vehicle telematics, driver tax compliance and more.

Sustainable Fleet Solutions for a Greener Future

As businesses increasingly prioritize sustainability, Wheels is committed to
helping you transition to an environmentally friendly fleet.  We offer
comprehensive fleet management solutions for electric vehicles (EVs),
including:

     * Vehicle Selection and Acquisition: Our experts can guide you
       through the process of selecting and acquiring the right EVs for
       your needs.
     * Charging Infrastructure: We can help you plan and implement the
       necessary charging infrastructure to support your EV fleet.
     * Driver Training: We provide comprehensive driver training programs
       to ensure your team is prepared to operate and maintain EVs.
     * Software Integration: Our fleet management software seamlessly
       integrates with EVs, providing real-time monitoring, charging
       status updates, and performance data.
